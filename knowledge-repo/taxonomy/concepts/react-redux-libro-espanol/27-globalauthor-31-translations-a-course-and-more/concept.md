GlobalAuthor works with books which are inMarkua or Leanpub Flavoured Markdown(in any writing mode).

If your book is in Leanpub Flavoured Markdown or Markua 0.10,we will convert it to Markua 0.31as part of the service.

The reason why we do this is that by defaultwe add an index to your book, and Markua 0.31 is the only dialect of Markdown which supports index entries.

Markua 0.31 is the most powerful and flexible dialect of Markdown, and it is the best way to write books on Leanpub.

If your book is inWord, EPUB or PDF, you will need to convert it toMarkuafirst. We can give you a custom quote on this if you need help with the conversion. The best thing to do if this describes your situation is tobook a Zoom call on our Calendly pageto discuss how GlobalAuthor would fit with one or more of your books!

IndexAI is our AI service where we use AI to create the index for your book. (If you're under 25 years old: printed books typically have an index at the back of book, which lists all the important topics in the book, and the page numbers where they can be found. You can think of it as a bunch of links you can't click on.) Having an index is a sign of a professional-quality book, and it makes your book more useful to readers.

After we create and review the index, you can optionally review it and request or make changes, before we proceed. (The way indexes are created is that index entries are added inline in your manuscript, per theMarkua spec.)

Since we create the index first, the index will be in your Amazon book, and translated in all your Leanpub translations.

IndexAI cannot be purchased separately. IndexAI and AccessibilityPro can occasionally cause rare issues when your book is translated into 31 languages, and we resolve those issues manually with human QA. Because of this hands-on work, the only way we sell IndexAI and AccessibilityPro is as part of GlobalAuthor Max.

AccessibilityPro is our newest "AI plus human work" service. First, we use AI to add alt text to all your images for accessibility purposes. Next, we do manual work to ensure that the EPUB we generate is accessible, and that it passes the Ace by DAISY accessibility checker with no reported issues.

Since we do this before any translations, the alt text is also translated in all your translations.

AccessibilityPro cannot be purchased separately; it can only be purchased as part of GlobalAuthor Max.
